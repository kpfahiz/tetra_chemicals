Automobile Chemicals

Premium fluids and agents for maximum engine performance, longevity, and aesthetics.

Antifreeze Coolant

Advanced thermal management fluid to prevent engine overheating and freezing. Manufactured in concentrate ranges from 5% to 50%, featuring OAT technology for extended life and superior corrosion protection.

Diesel Exhaust Fluid

Reduces emissions in diesel engines, meeting strict environmental compliance. Certified to ISO 22241, available in both AUS 32 (standard) and AUS 40 (marine/high SCR efficiency) grades.

Lubricants & Oil

High-viscosity oils to minimize engine friction and wear over time. Engineered with advanced additive packages to maintain viscosity integrity under extreme pressure and high temperatures.

Battery Water

Purified compounds to ensure maximum lifespan of automotive batteries. Features highly purified deionized water, ensuring zero mineral contamination for optimal electrolyte performance.

Windshield Washer

Clear visibility fluids optimized to break down grime and bug residue. Concentrated formula with rapid-action surfactants that dissolve road film, tree sap, and insect residue instantly.

Cleaning Agents

Heavy-duty degreasers to remove stubborn oil and carbon deposits. Industrial-strength heavy-duty formula, engineered to emulsify grease on contact for hassle-free engine cleaning.

Dashboard / Tyre Polish

Restorative polish that protects interiors and rubber from UV damage. High silicone-based formula that restores showroom shine while creating a durable UV barrier against cracking.

Autowash Liquid

High-foam pH-balanced shampoo that protects wax coatings while washing. Thick, luxurious foam suspension safely lifts dirt without stripping existing wax or sealant layers.

Battery Acid

Precision-formulated electrolyte solutions with controlled specific gravity for maximum battery efficiency and starting power.
